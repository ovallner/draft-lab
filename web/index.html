Draft experiments
Your strategies
JSON array. Policies: live (drafts by calling the production recommendation engine each turn — authoritative, slower; the option to compare against), fit (competent ADP + nine-category value + positions/balance baseline), adp_stochastic (pooled ADP manager baseline), adp (deterministic best-available ADP, zero variance), playoff, auto_punt. Streaming: aggressive, observed, injury_only, off. Change punts, playoff_weight and risk to compare builds. `live` ignores punts/risk here and uses the engine's own build; other policies are transparent fast heuristics.
Opponent draft models
Runs on adp_stochastic as the primary model (sample it against at least one other). The recommendation is based on the primary model and required to be stable across the rest. Models are never averaged. Strict ADP is a compressed-variance baseline only: it approximately halves league spread. Experimental personas may overfit one season.
Override individual managers
Map canonical manager names to a model or strategy object, e.g. {"Shiven":"adp_deterministic"}. Manager strategy objects can specify punts and streaming.

 
Season assumptions
Three IL slots and 13 active/bench roster slots per team. Injury episodes, isolated absences and performance noise are synthetic and paired between strategies. Managers compete for one shared player pool and budgets. Verify waiver processing and lock interpretation in Yahoo.
Scoring calendar · 20 league weeks from Yahoo (verified)
